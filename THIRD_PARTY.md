# Third-party dependencies

- JavaFX 17.0.12 / OpenJFX: GPL v2 with Classpath Exception. Maven artifacts: `org.openjfx:javafx-controls`, `javafx-graphics`, `javafx-base`, `javafx-swing`. Project: https://openjfx.io/ . Source: https://github.com/openjdk/jfx/tree/17.0.12 . The jars retain their bundled notices.
- JGraphT 1.5.2: dual licensed EPL 2.0 / LGPL 2.1 or later; used under EPL 2.0. Provides the A* implementation. Project/source: https://github.com/jgrapht/jgrapht/tree/jgrapht-1.5.2 . License: https://github.com/jgrapht/jgrapht/blob/jgrapht-1.5.2/license-EPL.txt . Includes transitive dependencies jheaps 0.14 (Apache 2.0) and apfloat 1.10.1 (LGPL 2.1 or later); their original jars are included unchanged.
- JUnit 5.10.3: EPL 2.0, development/test only, not shipped in the playable runtime.
- The Windows portable release includes a reduced Eclipse Temurin OpenJDK 17 runtime, GPL v2 with Classpath Exception. Its `runtime/legal` directory contains the license and third-party notices for the included modules. Corresponding upstream source is available at https://github.com/adoptium/temurin17-binaries/releases and https://github.com/openjdk/jdk17u .

The maze generation concept and driving behavior are derived from the user-supplied prototype. Original authorship and any upstream licensing of that prototype remain with its authors. This delivery does not assign a new license to the user's project. Some artwork and every character voice line are AI-generated rather than drawn or recorded: the portraits, chibi figures and scene illustrations come from an image generator, and the 42 voice clips are synthesised with GPT-SoVITS over a public generic voice model. README.md lists what came from where. Everything else in the presentation layer - the campus and mecha graphics, the sound effects and the music - is produced programmatically in this repository.

The campus cartoon theme uses the user's supplied illustration as a style reference. Buildings, trees, sports fields, tank portraits and control icons are original JavaFX Canvas drawings in `CampusArtwork.java`; the reference image itself is not distributed in the playable package. Fonts use the installed Microsoft YaHei and system fallbacks; no font files are redistributed.

Enemy evasion behavior was informed by an earlier prototype supplied by a team member, particularly its `EnemyAi` and `Config` classes: approaching-projectile detection, perpendicular clearance probes, held dodge direction, post-shot relocation and reverse recovery. The implementation is adapted to this project's AABB collision and simulation model. That prototype arrived without an upstream repository URL or license file; no new license or upstream attribution is inferred here.
